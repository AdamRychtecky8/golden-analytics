# Challenge brief (Golden Analytics, Full Stack Engineer Challenge)

Received Oct 26, 2026 from Golden Analytics recruiting. Due Nov 9, 2026. Personal contact details removed.

Most business users can't easily get value from their own data. The marketing analyst who needs last month's regional numbers has to file a ticket and wait two days. The sales ops manager tracking deal velocity is working from a spreadsheet that's cumbersome to understand. The business owner who wants to understand their top customers has no idea where to even start.

Golden Analytics is building toward "Canva for data" — a world where any business user can understand, explore, and act on their data, without needing an engineer, a BI tool license, or a SQL degree. The people we're building for are not technical. They are smart, they have real questions, and they are currently stuck.

**Your challenge:** given a set of data, create an interactive web application that allows a non-technical-but-motivated user to get some value from the data. Produce a web application (single page or client/server) application to demonstrate your idea.

What capability to demonstrate is up to you. It should be something that is interactive, not just a static presentation of data. You can use calls to AI or other external services if you like, or build out something purely interactive. We are looking for someone who will apply good product sense and judgement to an open-ended problem, rather than just build the most obvious thing. When deciding what to do, think about choosing one of the fundamental operations that people need to do with data: cleaning, transforming, visualizing/storytelling, etc.

Suggested datasets (or use data of your own choosing; a great solution won't be tied to a single dataset, but have some generality to it):
- MovieLens
- 120 Years of Olympic History
- Messy Employee Dataset
- Bookshop data

We expect you to use AI in completing this challenge — please keep track of your major prompts and how you guided the AI during the project. We also don't expect you to get everything to polished completion — expect to spend about 45 minutes on the challenge, and keep track of what you would do if you had extra time to spend on it. Produce something well-engineered even if it's not complete.

Your AI agent will certainly have a strong opinion on what to build. You'll have to demonstrate that you guided the AI to build something that YOU wanted to build (and why you wanted to build it), not something that it wanted to build.

**Deliverables**
- A short video (5 minutes) walking through your solution and why you designed it this way.
- Your application code with instructions on how to run it
- Any supporting data files that you used, if not the provided data
- A README, written by you (not the AI):
  - Why you built what you built
  - How you used AI to build it
  - What you would do if you had more time
