# Golden Analytics — company context

Paraphrased from public coverage (researched Oct 26, 2026). Sources at bottom.

## Basics
- Founded 2026 by Francois Ajenstat (~30 years in BI: Cognos, Microsoft, 13 years at Tableau including 7+ as CPO, then CPO at Amplitude).
- Out of stealth April 7, 2026 with a $7M seed co-led by NEA and Madrona. Seattle/Bellevue.
- Pitch: Tableau's analytical depth + Canva's design sensibility + Cursor's AI-native workflow.

## Product ideas to internalize
- **Slider of Autonomy:** the user sets how much the AI does vs. how much they do, from AI standing by to AI on autopilot. The AI's work stays visible.
- **AI removes drudgery; humans keep judgment.** AI handles prep, formatting, repetitive mechanics. AI is an accelerator, not in charge.
- **AI-native, not bolted on:** AI works in the canvas, on data in place.
- **No chat-with-your-data bots:** the founder has said he dislikes AI chatbots as the interface for data.
- **Speed to value:** a business user should go from a table to a working dashboard in about two minutes.
- **Storytelling + design** united with autonomy in visual analytics.
- **Precision and validation:** BI needs correctness that general-purpose vibe-coded dashboards don't guarantee.
